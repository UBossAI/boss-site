\section{Overview}

UBOSS LLC (``UBOSS,'' ``Company,'' ``we,'' ``us,'' or ``our'') provides operational automation services for small businesses in the trades and service industries. As part of our services, we send transactional SMS text messages on behalf of ourselves and our business clients (``Tenants'') using Twilio-powered messaging infrastructure.

This SMS Messaging Policy describes the terms and conditions governing text messages sent through UBOSS-registered phone numbers. By providing your phone number and consenting to receive text messages, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

\section{Program Description}

UBOSS sends transactional SMS messages to two categories of recipients:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item \textbf{Business Clients and Staff:} Internal notifications alerting business owners and team members of new customer inquiries, job assignments, and operational updates.
    \item \textbf{End-User Customers:} Appointment confirmations, service request acknowledgments, post-service follow-ups, and review requests sent to customers on behalf of UBOSS Tenant businesses.
\end{enumerate}

All messages are strictly \textbf{transactional} in nature. UBOSS does not send promotional, marketing, or advertising messages via SMS.

\section{Consent and Opt-In}

End users opt in to receive SMS messages through the following methods:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item \textbf{Web Form:} Customers submit a service request through an online form that includes a phone number field and a consent checkbox agreeing to receive SMS updates related to their service request.
    \item \textbf{Verbal Consent:} Business clients may verbally confirm consent for their staff or customers to receive transactional messages.
    \item \textbf{Via Text:} Users may initiate contact by texting an UBOSS-registered number, which constitutes consent to receive a response.
\end{itemize}

Consent is limited to transactional communications related to the service requested. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

\section{Message Frequency}

Message frequency varies based on the nature of the service engagement and automation workflow. Users will only receive messages directly related to a service they have requested or an appointment they have scheduled.

\section{Message and Data Rates}

\textbf{Standard message and data rates may apply} depending on your wireless carrier and mobile plan. UBOSS is not responsible for any charges incurred by your carrier.

\section{Opt-Out Instructions}

You may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time.

\begin{center}
    \textbf{To stop receiving messages, reply \textsf{STOP} to any message from an UBOSS-registered number.}
\end{center}

Upon receipt of a STOP request:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item You will receive a single confirmation message acknowledging your opt-out.
    \item No further messages will be sent from that number.
    \item Your opt-out applies only to the specific number and campaign from which the request was made.
\end{itemize}

To resume messages after opting out, reply \textbf{START}.
