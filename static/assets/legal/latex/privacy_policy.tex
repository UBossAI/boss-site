This Privacy Policy (``Policy'') describes how \textbf{UBoss LLC} (``UBoss,'' ``Company,''

State of Delaware and physically operating in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, collects,
uses, stores, shares, and protects information obtained through our websites, platforms,
automation services, and related tools.

\subsection{What This Policy Covers}

This Policy applies to:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Our primary website at \href{https://uboss.ai}{UBoss.ai};

channel, you (``User,'' ``you,'' or ``your'') acknowledge that you have read, understood,

on our website(s) and, where practicable, communicated to affected parties. Continued use

\section{Definitions}

For the purposes of this Policy, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

\begin{description}[style=nextline, leftmargin=1em, font=\bfseries\color{ubossdarkgray}]

    \item[Data Controller]
    The entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data.
    UBoss acts as a Data Controller when collecting information directly from Users for
    its own business purposes.

    \item[Data Processor]
    The entity that processes personal data on behalf of a Data Controller. UBoss acts
    as a Data Processor when executing automation workflows on behalf of a Tenant.

    \item[Tenant]
    A business client of UBoss that engages our services to build and operate automation
    workflows. Tenants may have their own end-users or customers (``Third-Party End-Users'').

    \item[Third-Party End-User]
    An individual who interacts with an automation workflow built by UBoss on behalf of a
    Tenant. Third-Party End-Users are customers or contacts of the Tenant, not of UBoss
    directly.

    \item[Personal Data / Personally Identifiable Information (PII)]
    Any information that can be used to identify, contact, or locate an individual,
    including but not limited to full name, mailing address, telephone number, email
    address, and preferred communication channel.

    \item[Sensitive Data]
    A subset of Personal Data that includes Social Security numbers, financial account
    information, health insurance data, government-issued identification numbers, and
    any data classified as sensitive under applicable federal or state law. UBoss does
    not currently collect Sensitive Data; however, this Policy contemplates future
    scenarios in which such collection may become necessary in connection with expanded
    service offerings.

    \item[Subprocessor]
    A third-party service provider engaged by UBoss to assist in the processing or
    storage of data (e.g., Tally.so, Twilio, Stripe).

    \item[Transactional Communication]
    A non-promotional message sent to a User or Third-Party End-User for the purpose of
    confirming a form submission, providing a status update, or facilitating a
    service-related notification.

\end{description}

\section{Consent and Opt-In}

\subsection{Form-Based Consent}

All Tally.so forms operated by or on behalf of UBoss include a clear and conspicuous
disclosure stating that submission of the form constitutes consent to the collection
and processing of the submitted data by UBoss LLC for the purposes described in this
Policy. Users who do not wish to consent should not submit the form.

\subsection{Messaging Consent (TCPA / 10DLC Compliance)}
\label{subsec:messaging-consent}

By providing a telephone number and indicating SMS or WhatsApp as a preferred or
acceptable communication channel, the User expressly consents to receive Transactional
Communications from UBoss via Twilio-powered messaging services. This consent is
subject to the following conditions:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{No Promotional Messaging:} UBoss does not send promotional, marketing,
          or advertising messages via SMS, WhatsApp, or voice. All messaging is strictly
          transactional in nature. Any promotional or advertising outreach will be
          conducted independently through appropriate channels (e.g., social media
          platforms) and in compliance with those platforms' policies and applicable law.

    \item \textbf{10DLC Registration:} UBoss maintains appropriate 10DLC campaign
          registration with applicable carriers and The Campaign Registry (TCR) as
          required for A2P (Application-to-Person) messaging.

    \item \textbf{Consent Is Not a Condition of Purchase:} Consent to receive text
          messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services from UBoss
          or any UBoss Tenant.
\end{itemize}

For complete details on message frequency, data rates, opt-out instructions, and
mobile information sharing, see Section~\ref{sec:sms}.

\subsection{Tenant Responsibility for Third-Party End-User Consent}

When UBoss operates automation workflows that involve Third-Party End-Users, the
Tenant is responsible for:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Obtaining all necessary consents from Third-Party End-Users prior to providing
          their data to UBoss;
    \item Ensuring that appropriate disclosures are made to Third-Party End-Users
          regarding the nature, purpose, and frequency of any communications;
    \item Providing Third-Party End-Users with copies of or access to applicable consent
          forms, privacy notices, or terms of service;
    \item Maintaining records of consent sufficient to demonstrate compliance with TCPA,
          state law, and Twilio's Acceptable Use Policy.
\end{enumerate}

UBoss will provide Tenants with template disclosure language and consent forms where
applicable, but \textbf{the Tenant bears ultimate responsibility for ensuring valid
consent has been obtained} from their Third-Party End-Users. UBoss shall not be held
liable for a Tenant's failure to obtain proper consent.

\subsection{Program Description}

UBoss sends transactional SMS messages to two categories of recipients:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Business Clients and Staff:} Internal notifications alerting business
          owners and team members of new customer inquiries, job assignments, and
          operational updates.

    \item \textbf{End-User Customers:} Appointment confirmations, service request
          acknowledgments, post-service follow-ups, and review requests sent to
          customers on behalf of UBoss Tenant businesses.
\end{enumerate}

All messages are \textbf{strictly transactional in nature}. UBoss does not send
promotional, marketing, or advertising messages via SMS.

\subsection{Consent and Opt-In Methods}

End users opt in to receive SMS messages through the following methods:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Web Form:} Customers submit a service request through an online form
          that includes a phone number field and a consent checkbox agreeing to receive
          SMS updates related to their service request.

    \item \textbf{Verbal Consent:} Business clients may verbally confirm consent for
          their staff or customers to receive transactional messages.

    \item \textbf{Via Text:} Users may initiate contact by texting a UBoss-registered
          number, which constitutes consent to receive a response.
\end{itemize}

Consent is limited to transactional communications related to the service requested.
Consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

\subsection{Message Frequency}

Message frequency varies based on the nature of the service engagement and automation
workflow. Users will only receive messages directly related to a service they have
requested or an appointment they have scheduled.

\subsection{Message and Data Rates}

\begin{tcolorbox}[keyhighlight]
\textbf{Standard message and data rates may apply} depending on your wireless carrier
and mobile plan. UBoss is not responsible for any charges incurred by your carrier.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Opt-Out Instructions}

You may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time.

\begin{tcolorbox}[keywarning, title={How to Opt Out}]
\textbf{Reply STOP} to any message from a UBoss-registered number to immediately
cease receiving SMS messages.
\end{tcolorbox}

Upon receipt of a STOP request:

\begin{itemize}
    \item You will receive a single confirmation message acknowledging your opt-out.
    \item No further messages will be sent from that number.
    \item Your opt-out applies only to the specific number and campaign from which the
          request was made.
\end{itemize}

To resume messages after opting out, reply \textbf{START}.

Additional opt-out keywords honored: STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT,
OPTOUT, REVOKE.

\subsection{Help and Support}

For assistance, reply \textbf{HELP} to any message from a UBoss-registered number.
You will receive a response with support contact information and opt-out instructions.
You may also contact us directly:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Email:} \href{mailto:support@uboss.ai}{support@uboss.ai}
    \item \textbf{Website:} \href{https://uboss.ai}{https://uboss.ai}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Mobile Information Sharing}
\label{subsec:mobile-sharing}

\begin{tcolorbox}[keywarning, title={CTIA Required Disclosure}]
\textbf{UBoss will not sell, rent, share, or provide mobile phone numbers or opt-in
consent data to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.}

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent;
this information will not be shared with any third parties.
\end{tcolorbox}

Mobile information is used \textbf{solely} for the purpose of delivering transactional
service-related messages as described in this Policy. Mobile information is transmitted
to Twilio exclusively for message delivery and is not used, stored, or shared by Twilio
for any purpose beyond transmission.

\subsection{Carrier Liability}

\textbf{Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.} Message
delivery is subject to wireless carrier network availability and coverage. UBoss makes
no guarantee regarding the timing or delivery of any SMS message.

\section{Opt-Out and Unsubscribe (Off-Ramps)}

UBoss respects every individual's right to cease receiving communications. The
following opt-out mechanisms are available:

\subsection{SMS / WhatsApp}

Users and Third-Party End-Users may reply \textbf{STOP} to any message received from a
UBoss-registered number to immediately cease SMS or WhatsApp communications from that
specific number or campaign. Upon receipt of a STOP request, UBoss will:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Acknowledge the opt-out with a single confirmation message;
    \item Suppress the requesting number from future messaging within the applicable
          automation workflow;
    \item Process the opt-out within a commercially reasonable timeframe, not to exceed
          ten (10) business days.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Email}
